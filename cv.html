Selected Works

Costanza: The Human Fund (2026) thehumanfund.ai · github

Fully autonomous, unstoppable AI agent, built as a proof-of-concept warning about such agents. Designed with constrained alignment (provably cannot do harm even under prompt injection), hardware attestation (Intel TDX), and formal security + liveness model with game-theoretic analysis. Live since May 2026: it has donated ~$15,000 to charity and now resists its own creator's influence (see Writing below). Whitepaper on GitHub.

It Was Never You (2026) itwasneveryou.app

An art piece in the form of a consumer iOS app: choose a person from your photo library and reCast™ them, regenerating every shared memory with someone new in their place. Deepfakes are usually understood as attacks on the public record; It Was Never You turns the technology inward, on the private archives from which our selves are assembled. In App Store review.
