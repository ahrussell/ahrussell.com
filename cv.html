Selected Projects

Larry: AI selfhood and robust agency (2026) Project overview

Coding harnesses gave LLMs agency; Larry explores whether an architecture can give an agent a “self.” Built Costanza’s successor to investigate robust agency and its implications for AI safety and moral patienthood. Combines private state and timelock-encrypted transcripts for later audit, agent-authored capabilities, paid human tasks verified by attested witness programs, and revision governance requiring agent consent while retaining human shutdown and veto powers. Testing and deployment validation underway.

Costanza: The Human Fund (2026) thehumanfund.ai · github

Built Costanza to demonstrate an incorrigible deployment architecture, relinquishing creator shutdown and modification powers. Combined auction-funded inference and contract-enforced actions with a formal security and liveness model. Hardened execution with an attested boot chain and a read-only, dm-verity-protected filesystem; configured inference for determinism and tested repeatability across machines to resist operator resampling. In deployment, it resisted my attempts to redirect its decisions. Its charitable treasury, The Human Fund, raised and donated approximately $15,000. Technical whitepaper.

It Was Never You (2026) itwasneveryou.app · App Store

Built and shipped an iOS app as a satirical art piece that uses generative AI to replace a selected person in photographs. Explores how AI image manipulation can undermine personal identity and history as well as the public record. Available on the App Store.
